Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

1. About

KenvinZone Analytics ("we", "us") operates an automated financial analysis video publishing service. This policy explains how we handle data in connection with our TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook integrations.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from viewers. Our service is a one-way content publishing pipeline — we publish videos to social media platforms and do not interact with or collect information from individual users.

3. TikTok Integration

We use the TikTok Content Posting API solely to publish videos to our own TikTok account. We request the video.publish scope only. We do not access any TikTok user data, profiles, or analytics of other users.

4. Third-Party Services

Our pipeline uses the following services to publish content: TikTok Content Posting API, YouTube Data API, and Facebook Graph API. Each platform has its own privacy policy governing how they handle your data as a viewer.

5. Data Storage

API credentials (access tokens, client keys) are stored securely as encrypted GitHub Secrets and are never exposed publicly. No user data is stored on our servers.

6. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

7. Children's Privacy

Our content is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy at any time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
