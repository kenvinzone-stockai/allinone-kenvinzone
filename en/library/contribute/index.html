Contribution guidelines

How the KenvinZone Trading Library accepts and reviews articles from invited researchers and educators.

Today every article is written and reviewed by KenvinZone. The library is not open for self-publishing: financial education needs review.

Workflow

  1. Draft

    The author writes to the template and frontmatter standard. Drafts may be written by a person or an AI.

  2. Technical review

    Definitions, formulas, chart examples, data sources and every AURORA statement are checked.

  3. Editorial review

    Tone, structure, concept links and the trust rules are checked.

  4. Publish

    KenvinZone gives final approval and publishes the Vietnamese version, then the English translation.

Editorial rules

  • No profit promises.
  • No fake broker results or unsourced statistics.
  • State data sources; label historical examples as historical.
  • Disclose any commercial conflict of interest.
  • Articles must stay useful to readers who do not use AURORA.
  • Every article needs KenvinZone approval before publishing.
