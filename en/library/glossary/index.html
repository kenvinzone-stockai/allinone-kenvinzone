Trading glossary

The KenvinZone trading glossary: short definitions, full articles and how AURORA uses each concept.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ#

#

1st BREAK
The first breakout in the direction of a newly confirmed trend cycle. The core AURORA setup.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
2nd BREAK
The second breakout in the direction of the same trend cycle, after the 1st BREAK. Tracked separately because the first is sometimes swept.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro

A

AbsorptionHấp thụ
Unusually high volume with little price progress or a long wick: buying (selling) is being absorbed by the other side, usually a warning sign.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
ATRAverage True Range
The average price range over the last N bars. Used to measure volatility and to set stop distances that fit the market.
In AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Avg RAverage R per trade
Average result per trade measured in R. Fairer than total R when comparing groups with different trade counts.
In AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro

B

Bar-close confirmation
A signal is confirmed only once the bar has closed. It does not change after the close, but it can appear later than intrabar.
In AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
BOSBreak of Structure
Price closes beyond the previous structural high (or low) in the direction of the current trend, showing continuation.
Breakeven (BE)Hòa vốn
Moving the stop to the entry price so the remaining position cannot lose (before costs and slippage).
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Breakout
Price moves beyond an important level or zone. A breakout is only useful with explicit confirmation and invalidation rules.

C

CHoCHChange of Character
The first structure break against the prior trend, often read as an early sign of a reversal.
CVDCumulative Volume Delta
The running total of Delta over many bars, showing whether aggressive buying or selling dominates. On TradingView it is usually estimated from lower timeframes.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro

D

Delta
The difference between aggressive buying and selling volume. On TradingView it is usually estimated from lower-timeframe bars, not native bid / ask order-flow data.
In AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Drawdown
The decline from an equity peak to the following trough. It shows how deep a losing stretch you must be able to sit through.

E

Entry Filter Profile
The AURORA Pro setting that decides how selective setups are: minimum score and maximum Break stop distance. Kept separate from trade management.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro

F

F-SLFixed stop loss
The fixed stop of a trade, set at entry by the setup rules. It is the basis for 1R and position size.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Family Score
AURORA's 0–100 scoring made of six independent families (Trend, Momentum, Structure, Volatility, Flow, Execution), combined by weight.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
FVGFair Value Gap
A price gap in a three-candle pattern where the wicks of candles one and three do not overlap, showing a fast one-sided move.
In AURORA Pro

G

Ghost tradeLệnh ma
A setup blocked by a filter but still tracked in the background as if it had been taken, with the same stop, targets and exit rules. Used to measure what the filter is really worth.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro

I

IFVGInverse Fair Value Gap
An FVG that price closes through and that flips role: former resistance becomes support, or the reverse.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro
In-sample (IS)
The data period used to choose and tune parameters. Results on it usually look better than reality.
Read the full article

K

Kenvin Super TrailingĐường Trailing
AURORA's step-shaped dynamic stop line, built from pivot points and ATR. It only moves with the trend and flips when price closes through it.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro

L

Limit orderLệnh chờ
An order that fills only at your price or better. In exchange, it may never fill if price does not come back.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
LiquidityThanh khoản
On a chart, usually areas where resting orders cluster, such as stops below obvious lows or above obvious highs. Price often sweeps them before moving on.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro

M

MSSMarket Structure Shift
Price closes beyond the latest high (or low) of the pullback, confirming momentum has returned in the expected direction.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro
MTFMulti-timeframe
Looking at several timeframes together. Higher-timeframe data is only reliable when taken from closed higher-timeframe bars.
In AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro

O

OIOpen Interest
The total number of open derivative contracts. Rising price with rising OI often signals new money entering.
In AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
OOSOut-of-sample
Data not used when choosing parameters. OOS results show whether a configuration works beyond the data it was tuned on.
Order Block
In the common definition, the last opposite-direction candle before a strong displacement, used as a zone price may revisit.
In AURORA Pro

P

POCPoint of Control
The price level with the most traded volume over a period, from a volume profile.
Position sizingTính khối lượng
Sizing a trade from the amount you accept to lose and the distance to the stop, instead of trading a fixed size.
In AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Profit Factor
Gross profit divided by gross loss. Above 1 means profitable on the tested data, but read it with sample size, drawdown and costs.

R

R-Multiple
A trade result expressed as a multiple of the initial risk. A full stop-out is −1R; a gain of twice the risk is +2R.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Reference volumeVolume tham chiếu
Volume taken from a related market with real volume, for example gold futures used for a gold CFD. Useful, but not the CFD's own volume.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Runner
A small part of the position kept after the final take-profit, with a stop trailing the trend, to capture long runs.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro
RVOLRelative Volume
Current volume compared with its normal level. Measuring against the same time of day removes session effects.
In AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro

S

Setup QualityBreakQ · SunnyQ
A setup-specific quality score for BREAK or SUNNY, measuring compression then expansion, candle decisiveness, path efficiency and location in the trend. Statistics only by default.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro
SlippageTrượt giá
The difference between the price you intended and the price you actually got, usually unfavourable in fast or thin markets.
SMC Quality
A 0–100 quality score for SMC setups: displacement, zone or sweep quality, room to the opposing liquidity in R, and path efficiency.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro
SMC RETEST
An AURORA Pro SMC setup: after a Break, price returns into the displacement's FVG (or Order Block) and closes back beyond the zone midpoint in the trend direction.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro
Stop loss
The price at which the trade idea is considered wrong and the position is closed to cap the loss.
In AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Structural stopStoploss cấu trúc
A stop placed at the invalidation level of the price structure itself (beyond the zone or the sweep extreme), rather than at a general trailing line.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro
SUNNY
AURORA's with-trend continuation setup: entering when the market turns back in the main direction after a pullback.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
SweepLiquidity sweep
Price pierces an obvious high or low and then closes back inside, often after triggering resting orders around that level.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro

T

T-SLTrailing stop level
The dynamic stop following the current Trailing line, changing every bar. A reference for manual trade management.
In AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Take profit (TP)
A predefined profit-taking price. Often set as a multiple of risk, for example TP1 = 1R, TP2 = 2R.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Tick volume
The number of price changes within a candle, supplied by brokers for forex and many CFDs. Not real traded volume; use it only in relative terms.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro
Tier 3 · Institutional Quality
AURORA Pro's context layer: fresh FVGs, liquidity sweeps, CVD and absorption, plus suitability for holding a Runner. Statistics only by default.
Read the full articleIn AURORA Pro

V

VAH / VALValue Area High / Low
The upper and lower edges of the price area holding most of the traded volume (often 70%) in a volume profile.
Volume Quality (VQ)
A 0–100 score of whether a candle's volume supports the trade direction: RVOL strength, Delta direction, effort versus result, and volume expansion.
Read the full articleIn AURORA StandardIn AURORA Pro

W

Webhook
A way for TradingView to send an alert's message to a server or service URL, so a bot can process it.
