  2. What is the 1st BREAK?

    The 1st BREAK is the first time price breaks back through the Trailing line in the direction of a newly confirmed trend cycle, with a clear stop from the start.

  3. What is the 2nd BREAK?

    The 2nd BREAK is the second time price breaks back through the Trailing line in the direction of the same trend cycle, often after the first breakout was swept.

  4. What is SUNNY?

    SUNNY is a setup that enters when the market turns back in the main trend direction after a pullback, with price still on the right side of the Trailing line.

  5. What goes into a trade plan?

    A trade plan is a set of decisions locked in before entry: entry price, stop loss, size, where to take profit and when to exit early.

  6. Pullback Entry for long breakout candles

    Pullback Entry places a limit order at the 50% retracement of an overly long breakout candle instead of entering at that candle's close.
