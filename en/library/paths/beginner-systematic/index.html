Beginner to systematic trader

Build the decision foundation: trend, risk, sizing and how to measure results.

Beginner12 lessonsabout 1.6 h7 / 12 published

  1. What is a trend?

    A trend is price's dominant direction on a given timeframe, usually described by a sequence of rising highs and lows (up) or falling highs and lows (down).

  2. What is a stop loss?

    A stop loss is a price decided in advance at which the trade is closed because the idea is wrong, capping the loss at an acceptable amount.

  3. What is ATR?

    ATR (Average True Range) is the average price range over the last N bars, used to measure volatility and set stop distances that fit the market.

  4. What is R-Multiple?

    R-Multiple measures each trade as a multiple of its initial risk, so every trade sits on the same scale.

  5. How do you size a position?

    Position sizing works back from the money you accept to lose and the distance to the stop, instead of trading the same fixed size on every trade.

  6. Why wait for the bar to close before trusting a signal?

    Bar-close confirmation means treating a signal as real only once its candle has finished; the signal then no longer changes, at the cost of arriving a little later.

    A trade plan is a set of decisions locked in before entry: entry price, stop loss, size, where to take profit and when to exit early.
