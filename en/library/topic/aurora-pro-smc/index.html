AURORA Pro & SMC

Rule-based SMC, Setup Quality, Tier 3 and the Entry Filter Profile.

  • Entry Filter Profile: choosing how selective setups are

    The Entry Filter Profile is the AURORA Pro setting that decides which setups qualify for entry, through a minimum score and a maximum Break stop distance, independently of trade management.

    IntermediateAURORA Pro8 min read

  • What is an IFVG? When a price gap flips its role

    An IFVG (Inverse FVG) is an opposite-direction FVG that price closes through and that flips role: former resistance becomes support, and vice versa.

    AdvancedAURORA Pro7 min read

  • The Runner after TP2: keeping part of the trade for the trend

    The Runner is the 25% of the position kept after TP2, with its stop locked at TP1 and then trailing the Trailing line, to capture trends that run beyond 2R.

    AdvancedAURORA Pro8 min read

  • Setup Quality: scoring each BREAK and SUNNY on its own

    Setup Quality is a score for each individual BREAK or SUNNY setup, measuring compression then expansion, candle decisiveness, path efficiency and location in the trend.

    AdvancedAURORA Pro9 min read

  • SMC Quality: scoring SMC setups

    SMC Quality is a 0–100 score for each SMC setup, built from displacement, zone or sweep quality, room to the opposing liquidity in R, and path efficiency.

    AdvancedAURORA Pro6 min read

  • SMC RETEST: entering when price returns to the breakout's zone

    SMC RETEST is a setup that enters when price returns into the FVG (or Order Block) left by a Break, and a candle closes back beyond the zone midpoint in the trend direction.

    AdvancedAURORA Pro8 min read

  • Structural stops: placing the stop where the idea is really wrong

    A structural stop sits at the setup's own invalidation level, such as beyond an FVG zone or beyond a sweep's extreme, rather than at a general trailing line.

    AdvancedAURORA Pro6 min read

  • Tier 3: traces of large money flow around a setup

    Tier 3 is AURORA Pro's context layer, looking for traces of large money flow around a setup: fresh FVGs, liquidity sweeps, CVD and absorption, plus suitability for holding a Runner.

    AdvancedAURORA Pro9 min read

  • What is an SMC Sweep + MSS?

    A Sweep + MSS is when price sweeps the low (or high) of a pullback and then closes beyond the latest pullback high (or low), showing momentum has returned with the main trend.

    AdvancedAURORA Pro10 min read
