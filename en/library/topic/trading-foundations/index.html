Trading Foundations

Trend, timeframes, bar close: the concepts every trading decision rests on.

  • Why wait for the bar to close before trusting a signal?

    Bar-close confirmation means treating a signal as real only once its candle has finished; the signal then no longer changes, at the cost of arriving a little later.

    BeginnerAURORA StandardAURORA Pro6 min read

  • Multi-timeframe context

    Multi-timeframe analysis reads the trend on higher timeframes for context, then uses the trading timeframe to time entries in that direction.

    BeginnerAURORA StandardAURORA Pro6 min read

  • What is a trend?

    A trend is price's dominant direction on a given timeframe, usually described by a sequence of rising highs and lows (up) or falling highs and lows (down).

    BeginnerAURORA StandardAURORA Pro6 min read
