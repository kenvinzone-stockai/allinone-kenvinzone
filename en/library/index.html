Win rate only means something alongside the average win and loss; average R per trade (expectancy) combines both and decides whether a system has an edge.

Trading glossary

59 terms, each linking to the full article and to how AURORA uses the concept where relevant.

#ABCDEFGHIKLMOPRSTVW
ATRAverage True Range
The average price range over the last N bars. Used to measure volatility and to set stop distances that fit the market.
R-Multiple
A trade result expressed as a multiple of the initial risk. A full stop-out is −1R; a gain of twice the risk is +2R.
F-SLFixed stop loss
The fixed stop of a trade, set at entry by the setup rules. It is the basis for 1R and position size.
RVOLRelative Volume
Current volume compared with its normal level. Measuring against the same time of day removes session effects.
SweepLiquidity sweep
Price pierces an obvious high or low and then closes back inside, often after triggering resting orders around that level.
MSSMarket Structure Shift
Price closes beyond the latest high (or low) of the pullback, confirming momentum has returned in the expected direction.
FVGFair Value Gap
A price gap in a three-candle pattern where the wicks of candles one and three do not overlap, showing a fast one-sided move.
OOSOut-of-sample
Data not used when choosing parameters. OOS results show whether a configuration works beyond the data it was tuned on.
